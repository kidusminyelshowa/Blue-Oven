Blue Oven started as a two-person bakery: one at the oven, one at the counter. The recipes came from a grandmother's notebook in Gaziantep and haven't changed much since.
Today three rooms carry the same idea — bake small, serve fast, sit long. Tea is free with breakfast, and it always will be.
